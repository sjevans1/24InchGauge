# Public summons and administrative document source register

Research date: 7 November 2026. Scope: Kingston Lodge No. 1933 and English, Scottish and Irish lodges in Jamaica and the Cayman Islands.

## Result

No publicly accessible summons positively identified as Kingston Lodge No. 1933 was located in this search. This is a search outcome, not proof that no public copy exists.

Useful material found includes photographed summons extracts in a local Mark secretaries' seminar, a Scottish District meeting pack with notice and minutes, and an English Craft membership procedure. No verified local Irish Craft summons or exact mentee-module request form was recovered.

These are reference sources for product design. Publication online does not establish current approval, grant reuse rights, or make historical instructions current. None is an approved Kingston Craft template.

## Product decision after research

Each lodge will configure its branding and generate documents from built-in layouts and its own records. Existing summons, minutes and design templates are optional references, not prerequisites for onboarding or implementation. See [Master Development Blueprint, sections 5.1–5.2](MASTER_DEVELOPMENT_BLUEPRINT.md#51-lodge-branding-and-document-design). The research below informs optional design choices and later procedural checks; it does not establish a requirement for the founder or any lodge to supply a template.

## Sources with document content

### S1 — Local Mark secretaries' seminar, 31 October 2015

[Annual Returns and Reports — PDF](https://www.markdgljmci.com.jm/pdf/Secretaries-Annual-Returns-and-Reports.pdf)

- Publisher: District Grand Lodge of Mark Master Masons of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands.
- Scope: Mark and Royal Ark Mariner administration, distinct from Craft.
- PDF page 25: summons guidance.
- Pages 26–27: photographs of summons extracts. Page 26 shows an agenda dated 19 January 2015, reports, elections, a dues section and dress guidance; page 27 shows officers and meeting recurrence.
- Page 28: guidance on minutes and approval.
- Other pages illustrate returns, registration forms and document filing.
- The lodge name is not established by the photographed extracts reviewed. Do not attribute these to Kingston No. 1933.
- Use: historical layout and record-linking reference only. Do not import its constitutional provisions, deadlines, dues or officeholders into Craft defaults.

### S2 — Scottish District communication pack, 26 August 2023

[Public meeting booklet](https://fliphtml5.com/ogsus/bnfg/DGL_Communication_Aug_26_2023_booklet_-_Agenda%2C_Prev_Minutes%2C_Reports_%281%29/)

- A 47-page document for the District Grand Lodge of Jamaica, Scottish Constitution, publicly posted by the FlipHTML5 account pkgillings2004.
- Page 2: meeting notice dated 6 August 2023.
- Page 3: agenda.
- Pages 7–10: minutes of the preceding communication, held 25 February 2023.
- Subsequent sections include committee minutes and reports.
- This is an actual District administrative pack, not a daughter lodge's Craft summons.
- Use: connected notice, agenda, minutes and report structure. Its public posting has not been independently authenticated with the Secretariat.

### S3 — English District membership-processing procedure

[Procedure for Processing an Application for Membership into the Craft](https://www.dgljamci.org/projects/procedure-for-processing-an-application-for-membership-into-the-craft)

- Official English District website; no effective date established.

- Refers to forms, but the page itself is guidance rather than a blank summons or mentoring form.
- Use: evidence for linked candidate-to-meeting administration.
- Current applicability and exact deadlines must be checked before activating constitutional validation.

## Product implications — proposed, not constitutional rulings

1. Keep constitution, governing body, order and document level as separate settings. An English Mark document must not silently become an English Craft template; a District pack must not silently become a lodge summons.
2. Link each meeting's agenda, draft minutes, reports and attendance. Preserve issued summons and approved minutes as dated versions.
3. Make officer lists, venue, meeting recurrence, notices and financial information configurable. Any personal dues insert must be rendered only for its intended recipient.
4. Use confirmed membership events to prepare later drafts and mentoring tasks. A scheduled passing does not itself change a brother's degree or prove that a ceremony occurred.
5. Record each template's source, applicable lodge/order, revision, review status and approval. Separate visual design from enforceable procedural rules.
6. Use synthetic names and figures during development. Do not copy real candidate details, welfare reports, member balances or signatures into the public repository.

## Research gaps and later procedural checks

- Kingston, individual Scottish Craft and local Irish Craft summons were not recovered. Such examples remain optional comparison material; the application will create its own configurable designs.
- No exact local mentee-module request form was recovered. Internal requests and mentoring tasks can use the lodge's designer. Any prescribed external form must be checked against the receiving body's requirements before claiming it is accepted.
- Current constitutional and local procedural requirements remain to be checked separately for each profile. Approval of a visual layout is not constitutional validation.

The absence of existing documents does not block branded summons, agendas or minutes. No procedural profile should be labelled validated until its applicable evidence and review are recorded.
